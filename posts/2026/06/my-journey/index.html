    My Journey

    Something which few may know is that I initially started my studies studing architecture. After a few months, realizing it didn’t fit me, I dropped out to reconsider my career path. I ended up deciding I would apply for a two-year technical degree in Electrical Engineering. In the months leading up to the beginning of the next school year, I took up programming through various online courses- notably Harvard’s CS50 and the University of Michigan’s Python for Everybody. I little to no prior experience in coding.

    Something interesting which I came to realize once I started studying electrical engineering was that it was electrical engineering. Funnily enough, all I was looking for was to do engineering. What lead me to studying EE is that I researched which technical degree was best suited for being admitted to an engineering university in France - which turned out to be a degree in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science. Going into it, I barely even knew the difference between a current and a voltage. Despite this, I quickly came to like EE.
